Who We Are

Nurturing Elders with Sacred Respect & Compassion

Mamatha Old Age Home was founded with a single mission: to ensure no elder in or around Gudivada spends their twilight years in loneliness, hunger, or abandonment.

Safe & Peaceful Shelter

Clean living spaces, daily hygiene assistance, laundry support, and a peaceful garden area for morning walks and satsang.

Sattvic, Wholesome Meals

Three nutritious Andhra home-cooked meals every single day: freshly prepared idli/upma breakfasts, hearty lunches with dal, vegetables & curd, and light dinners.

Doctor Visits & Medicines

Regular health checkups by local Gudivada doctors, timely blood pressure/sugar monitoring, and continuous prescription medication support.
